# Demo video script (under 3:00)

Voice: Sarvam AI, bulbul:v3, speaker "shubh" (Indian English, male), generated by `scripts/make_voiceover.py`
into `web/video/`. The autopilot (`web/video/director.html`) plays each clip while it drives the site, so picture
and voice stay in step; with "Narration off" it keeps the same timing silently, for a voice recorded over it.
Satbir and Baljeet are two of the demo's made-up farmers. The voice is given "Sentinel two" so it does not spell the name out; the captions show Sentinel-2.

| # | Picture (autopilot) | Narration | Criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| s1 | Map opening: India, Delhi, the belt | Meet Satbir, a farmer in Patran. Last November, a fire on the next field went on his record, and the money promised for not burning came months late. Every November, burnt paddy straw from Punjab and Haryana chokes Delhi. Saakshi fixes how farmers are checked, and how they are paid. | Idea, impact |
| s2 | Play the season: burn patches and fire alerts appear day by day | This is where Satbir farms, on the Patiala Kaithal border, through the 2025 season. Red is ground that turned black after harvest, seen by Sentinel two. Yellow rings are NASA fire alerts. Most burns never raised one. On fields we labelled by hand, fire alerts wrongly flagged nine in a hundred clean fields. Saakshi, fewer than one. | Impact, execution |
| s3 | The story opens a field and runs the live check; zoom on the result and the AWS line | Open any field, and run the check. It reads every clear Sentinel two picture of the field, from AWS Open Data, on AWS Lambda. This one, Baljeet's, turned near-black on the sixth of November. A possible burn, and the smoke it released. | AWS, execution |
| s4 | Officer: evidence, note typed, Confirm burn; zoom on the checklist; the recovery notice | Saakshi never punishes on its own. Every possible burn goes to the village officer, with the pictures, the plant signal, and last season's record. When the officer confirms a burn, Saakshi drafts the recovery notice, and sends a link to return the advance. | Design, execution |
| s5 | Officer approves Satbir; zoom on the payment advice | Satbir's case. A fire alert came from the next field, but his own field shows no burn. The officer approves, and the money goes straight to his bank, through Cashfree Payouts, with a payment advice. Clean fields are paid automatically. | Execution |
| s6 | Satbir's page in Punjabi; his voice note; zoom on the passbook | Satbir sees the same pictures, in Punjabi, Hindi, Haryanvi, or English. Hears a voice note. Tracks every rupee. And can appeal, in one tap. | Design, impact |
| wa | WhatsApp: Satbir's chat, in a phone frame, with the bot's voice note, his spoken question and the spoken answer | Most farmers live on WhatsApp, so Saakshi does too. Satbir picks his state, and his name. His status comes in English and Punjabi. He taps Why, for the satellite pictures, and a voice note. Or asks, by voice. Saakshi hears his Punjabi, and answers in Punjabi. | Design |
| s7 | How it works: services light up; console screenshots; zoom on Bedrock | All of it runs on AWS. Lambda reads Sentinel two in place. DynamoDB keeps every decision, and its audit trail. CloudFront serves the app. And Amazon Bedrock, with the Strands Agents SDK, understands the farmer. The model picks a tool. It never decides. | AWS |
| s8 | Closing card: the numbers | Paid before harvest. Every field checked. Every burn, confirmed by a person. About nineteen kilograms of P M two point five kept out of the air, for every acre that stays clean. This is Saakshi. | Impact |

The WhatsApp scene is built by `scripts/make_wa_cut.py` from the phone recording: it cuts the clip (`wa.mp4`) and lays
these lines into the gaps between the voices worth hearing (`wa.mp3`). `make_voiceover.py` leaves it alone.
