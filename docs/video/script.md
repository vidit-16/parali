# Demo video script (under 3:00)

Voice: Amazon Polly, Kajal (Indian English, neural), generated by `scripts/make_voiceover.py` into `web/video/`.
The autopilot (`web/video/director.html`) plays each clip while it drives the site, so picture and voice stay in
step. The WhatsApp part is filmed on a phone and placed at the marker card.

| # | Picture (autopilot) | Narration | Criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| s1 | Map opening: India, Delhi, the belt | Every November, burnt paddy straw from Punjab and Haryana chokes Delhi. Farmers are paid to stop burning, but only after slow field visits, and the satellite fire alerts used as evidence often blame the wrong field. Saakshi fixes both. | Idea, impact |
| s2 | Play the season: burn patches and fire alerts appear day by day | This is the Patiala Kaithal border belt through the 2025 season. Red is ground that turned black after harvest, seen by Sentinel-2. Yellow rings are NASA fire alerts. Most burns never raised an alert: a field can burn and be ploughed between two passes. On fields we labelled by hand, fire alerts wrongly flagged nine in a hundred clean fields. Saakshi flagged fewer than one. | Impact, execution |
| s3 | The story opens a field and runs the live check; the result shows the AWS line | Open any field and run the check. It reads every clear Sentinel-2 picture of the field from AWS Open Data, on AWS Lambda. Here, on the sixth of November, the field turned near-black. A possible burn, and the smoke it released. | AWS, execution |
| s4 | Officer: the same case, evidence, note typed, Confirm burn; checklist ticks | Saakshi never punishes on its own. Every possible burn goes to the village officer, with the pictures, the plant signal and last season's record. When the officer confirms a burn, Saakshi drafts the recovery notice and sends a Razorpay link to return the advance. | Design, execution |
| s5 | Officer approves another case; checklist shows the Cashfree payout | When the officer approves, the balance goes straight to the farmer's bank through Cashfree Payouts, with a payment advice. Clean fields are paid automatically. | Execution |
| s6 | Farmer page in Punjabi: status, pictures, passbook with receipt | The farmer sees the same pictures, in Punjabi, Hindi, Haryanvi or English, hears a voice note, tracks every rupee, and can appeal in one tap. | Design, impact |
| wa | **Marker card: place the WhatsApp phone clip here** | Most farmers live on WhatsApp, so Saakshi does too. Pick or simply say your name. Your status arrives in English and your own language, with a voice note. Tap Why to see the satellite pictures, My money for your balance, or appeal with a voice note. | Design |
| s7 | How it works: each AWS service lights up | All of it runs on AWS. Lambda reads Sentinel-2 in place, DynamoDB keeps every decision and its audit trail, CloudFront serves the app, and Amazon Bedrock with the Strands Agents SDK understands the farmer. The model picks a tool. It never decides. | AWS |
| s8 | Closing card: the numbers | Paid before harvest. Every field checked. Every burn confirmed by a person. About nineteen kilograms of P M two point five kept out of the air, for every acre that stays clean. This is Saakshi. | Impact |

The WhatsApp narration (`wa.mp3`) is a draft: once the phone clip is filmed, its lines are re-timed to the clip.
