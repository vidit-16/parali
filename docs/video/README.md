# Recording the demo video

Everything on the web side plays by itself, with its narration. You record the screen, film one WhatsApp clip on
the phone, and join the two in Clipchamp (free with Windows). The rules: under 3:00, on YouTube (public or
unlisted), and the video must show AWS in use. The autopilot shows AWS three ways: the check result line ("read
from AWS Open Data, on AWS Lambda"), the How it works page, and the narration.

## 1. Web part (autopilot)

1. Open https://d5gpae5oilzws.cloudfront.net/video/director.html in Chrome on the laptop. Close other tabs and
   notifications; plug in the charger.
2. Press **Prepare** and wait for "Ready" (about a minute: it resets the demo to fresh cases).
3. Start recording the screen **with system sound**: Win + Alt + R (Xbox Game Bar), or OBS with Desktop Audio on.
4. Press **Start**. The page goes full screen; move the mouse to the screen edge. It runs about 2 minutes 50
   seconds and ends on the closing card. Stop recording.
5. Something went wrong in one part? Re-run from that scene: add `?from=s4` (or `s2`, `s5`, `s6`, `s7`) to the
   address, press Prepare first if the officer scenes are involved.

## 2. WhatsApp clip (phone)

Film with the iPhone's screen recording (Control Centre, long-press record, microphone on if you want your voice).
Keep it to about **16 seconds**: the narration under the card is already recorded and runs 16 seconds.

1. Send "change", tap **Punjab**, tap a farmer (the status arrives in English and Punjabi, with a voice note).
2. Tap **Why?** (the satellite pictures arrive).
3. Tap **My money**.
4. Optional: send a voice note asking about the claim.

If your clip runs longer, send me its timestamps ("0:03 tapped Punjab, 0:06 farmer, ...") and I will re-time the
WhatsApp narration to it.

## 3. Join them (Clipchamp)

1. Import both recordings. Put the screen recording on the timeline.
2. Find the "On WhatsApp" card (about 1:58). Put the phone clip on the track above it, starting where the card
   starts. Mute the phone clip, or keep 2-3 seconds of the bot's voice note and lower the narration there.
3. Resize the phone clip to fit the height of the frame; the dark card shows around it.
4. Check the total is under 3:00, export 1080p, upload to YouTube as unlisted, and open the link in a private
   window before submitting.
